New:
- Card statistics, turned on in the card view via the gear: every scan of your own card stores the balance and the last debit, only on the device.
- "My card": only a hash of the registered card is stored. Other cards still show their balance but are not stored.
- Payments, top-ups and Pfand returns are worked out from the scans. For unclear changes the app asks; Pfand returns can be assigned differently.
- Top-up and Pfand amounts can be set in the card settings.
- Statistics overview: spending and top-ups per week or month, spending by weekday, average per visit and balance over time.
- History of all entries, with delete and "Undo".
- Detailed statistics: payments are matched to the menus of all Mensen, including two dishes or the same dish twice. A dish is only saved automatically when exactly one fits and all menus are known. Otherwise the app asks and names missing Mensen.
- Extras like the salad bar, dessert by weight or drinks, plus your own extras and dishes.
- Dishes paid by weight are offered with the estimated weight ("approx. 470 g"), alone or together with fixed dishes and extras.
- Payments can be corrected: the search finds dishes of all Mensen and days, also parts of words. Menus stay stored for 14 days for this.
- Meals overview: dishes eaten most, Mensen, vegan and vegetarian share, average per meal. Dishes can be added to the favorites from there.
- Menu: the pasta buffet and the soup at UniCampus unten show "/100 g" next to the price, because they are paid by weight.
- Reminder on weekdays at the chosen time when the card wasn't scanned that day.
- Backup in the settings: export settings, favorites and card statistics as one file and import them again, with a confirmation and "Undo".
- The end of the statistics overview links to the website with statistics on all menus.

Changed:
- The card view has a gear for the card settings at the top; with statistics on, a button to the statistics joins it.
- With statistics on, the card view and the card popup show what was saved after a scan.

Fixed:
- A working fallback server is remembered across app restarts for the rest of the hour, which saves the wait for the unreachable main site.
