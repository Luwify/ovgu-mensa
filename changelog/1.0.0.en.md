The first public version of Mensa MD:
- Menus of all canteens and cafeterias of the Studentenwerk Magdeburg, shown instantly from storage and refreshed in the background
- Canteens as tabs, swipe between days; order and default canteen are up to you
- Filters for vegan, vegetarian, cake and favorites, and by additives and allergens
- Prices for your price group, CO₂ and water per portion
- Mark favorite dishes with a heart and, if you like, get notified when they are on the menu
- Show the balance of your Mensa card via NFC
- Themes: System, Soft, AMOLED and a custom theme; German and English
- Updates come from GitHub: the app finds and installs them itself
