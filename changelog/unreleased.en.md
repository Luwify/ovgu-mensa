Changed:
- The “What did you eat?” question is now one simple screen: the amount and day at the top, then up to three suggestions (combinations like “2× Chili” too) that save with one tap. One search finds everything else: dishes of every Mensa on that day, your own dishes, desserts and extras. If nothing fits, the search text can be created as a new dish right there (name, price, Mensa, tags).
- When an amount is still open after a pick, the question shows “… still open” and keeps searching. Dishes by weight, desserts and extras take what is left; every price can be tapped and changed. A rest can be saved as “Other” with one tap.
- “Later” keeps the question, “Don’t record” keeps only the amount. The former paths “Dish + extras”, “Several dishes”, “Choose an existing dish”, “Where did you eat?” and “Not on that day” are now part of this screen.
- The day of a payment can be changed in the question. When a scan comes more than a day after the previous one, the app asks for the dish as well and offers “Spread over several days”: each pick gets its day and becomes a payment of its own.
- Tapping a saved payment in the history opens the same screen with its items, to correct them.
- Extras are now stored one by one with their amount. Older entries with several extras split their shared amount evenly.
