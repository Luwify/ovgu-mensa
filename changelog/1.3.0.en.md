New:
- “High contrast” setting (Card tab): stronger colors and thicker lines in the statistics charts.
- Desserts: a generic “Dessert” by weight and your own desserts (e.g. Pudding, Rote Grütze), created from the search. A dish can be marked as “Dessert” when creating or editing it, by weight or at a fixed price. Desserts by weight cost 0.60 € per 100 g by default; the price can be changed with every pick and per dessert, e.g. for a special one. New “Desserts” section in the statistics: spending, estimated amount and a breakdown per dessert. Pudding, Rote Grütze and Dessert saved as extras so far become such desserts.
- “Avg. per day” in the statistics: spending divided by the number of days with at least one payment. “Avg. per visit” counts an amount of at least 2 payments as 2 visits.

Changed:
- The “What did you eat?” question is now one simple screen: the amount and day at the top, then up to three suggestions (combinations like “2× Chili” too) that save with one tap. One search finds everything else: dishes of every Mensa on that day, your own dishes, desserts and extras. If nothing fits, the search text can be created as a new dish right there (name, price, Mensa, tags).
- When an amount is still open after a pick, the question shows “… still open” and keeps searching. For dishes by weight, desserts and extras the app asks for the amount (what is left is filled in), so a salad and a pudding fit into one payment; every price can be tapped and changed. A rest can be saved as “Other” with one tap.
- New dishes can be created “priced by weight”, a dessert for example, optionally with a price per 100 g; the app then shows the approximate weight.
- “Later” keeps the question, “Don’t record” keeps only the amount. The former paths “Dish + extras”, “Several dishes”, “Choose an existing dish”, “Where did you eat?” and “Not on that day” are now part of this screen.
- The day of a payment can be changed in the question. When a scan comes more than a day after the previous one, the app asks for the dish as well and offers “Spread over several days”: each pick gets its day and becomes a payment of its own.
- With detailed statistics there is no separate “Several payments” question any more: when the balance dropped by more than the last debit, the total appears in the same question with the note “At least 2 payments since the last scan”; spreading over several days works as usual. Without detailed statistics the simple question stays.
- Your own extras (e.g. “Obstsalat”) can be turned into a dish of their own by tapping them, also by weight or as a dessert; every payment with that extra follows.
- Tapping a saved payment in the history opens the same screen with its items, to correct them.
- Extras are now stored one by one with their amount. Older entries with several extras split their shared amount evenly.
- The statistics charts have enough contrast in every color scheme, AMOLED included. Top-ups are drawn as outlined columns, so they differ from spending without relying on color.
- The weekly and monthly chart shows only spending at first; top-ups can be switched on with “Top-ups”, so a large top-up doesn’t squash the spending columns. The axis starts at the first week (or month) with data.

Fixed:
- “Record last debit?” only appeared on a card’s very first scan, so never for cards registered before version 1.2.3. The app now offers the unrecorded last debit until it is recorded or declined, also as a note on the card screen.
